The cause

Gift a talented student their dream degree.

BrightPath connects everyday donors with talented lower income (B40) students across Malaysia who have outstanding grades but not the means to continue past SPM. Your gift helps them cover fees, food, lodging and travel — every ringgit, guaranteed.

The public pool

Every one of these students has already been verified.

Citizenship, household income, grades and a place at a Malaysian institution are all checked before a profile appears here. Every student is identified by a code, never a name — sponsors receive progress updates without ever seeing who a student is.
