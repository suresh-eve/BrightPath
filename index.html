The gap no one talks about

A place isn't the same as a path.

Getting accepted is only the first hurdle. For a B40 household, the RM3,000 needed to actually show up — registration fees, food, a place to stay, the bus fare home — is often the harder one. Miss it, and a hard-earned place quietly lapses. Nothing about that student's ability changed; only their bank balance did.

BrightPath connects everyday donors directly to students in exactly that position — so a good SPM result is never wasted for lack of RM3,000.

Built alongside HalaTuju

We start where HalaTuju leaves off.

HalaTuju already solves the first half of this problem for free — its eligibility engine matches a student's SPM or STPM results against entry requirements across 1,300+ courses at 800+ public institutions, so no one settles for an expensive private college simply because they didn't know a subsidised public place was within reach.

But qualifying for a heavily-subsidised place doesn't make it free. BrightPath picks up exactly where that matching ends: once a student has a confirmed place and a coordinator-verified, anonymized profile, BrightPath opens the remaining RM3,000 gap up to donors like you — and reports funding status straight back to the HalaTuju team, so every verified student is tracked from application to graduation.
